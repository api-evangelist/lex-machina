This post was originally published in October 2024 and updated in September 2026. Lex Machina is proud to announce the release of enhancements to Litigation Footprint, a feature that offers an impressively broad range of party litigation data and that can combine searches across state, federal, and administrative courts into an easily digestible report. The new enhancements include a clarified report format identifying plaintiffs, defendants, and law firms for each where available.
